When will ALL reviews be finished?

How to read: the line is the projected queue size week by week if the recent pace continues. Going up = backlog growing forever; flattening = catching up; hitting zero = fully drained.

Queue depth over time

How to read: each point = ships waiting on that day. Rising = reviewers falling behind; falling = the backlog clearing. A flat line means queue in, queue out.

More charts (for the curious)

Decisions per day

How to read: tall bars = active review days, gaps = quiet days. Reviewers are volunteers — bursts of 5–10/day happen, so do quiet weeks.

Latency: days from submission to decision

How to read: the two humps are the two realities of this queue — a fast lane (claimed within hours) and a backlog lane (weeks). You can't tell from outside which one you're in.

Survival curve — % decided vs days waiting

How to read: the curve is your wait-time odds. At 30 days, 40% means: of 10 ships like yours, 4 were decided by day 30 — the other 6 kept waiting.

Arrivals per week

How to read: the last tall bars are the pre-cutoff rush, not an ongoing flow. Only projects first submitted on or before the cutoff are counted, so from the cutoff onward the only arrivals are resubmissions and second-pass ships of those projects.

Approval rates

How to read: among ships that got a decision, the share approved. Higher hours and higher levels don't hurt you — but the n= counts matter: trust cells with 20+ ships, not 5.
